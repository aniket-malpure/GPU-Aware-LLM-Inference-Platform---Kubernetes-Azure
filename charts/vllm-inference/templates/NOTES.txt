vLLM is starting. The first replica on a fresh GPU node can take 10-20 minutes
(node provisioning, image pull, weight download). Watch it with:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }} -w

Then test it:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "vllm.fullname" . }} 8000:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  curl -s localhost:8000/v1/chat/completions -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
    -d '{"model":"{{ .Values.model.servedName }}","messages":[{"role":"user","content":"Say hi"}],"max_tokens":32}'
